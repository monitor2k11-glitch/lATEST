EDM Cobalt Changes - Test Plan
1. Prerequisites and Test Data
1.	Agree the business dates to be tested: Business Date T, plus subsequent dates T+1, 
T+2, …, T+N.
2.	EDM to supply all input files for each agreed business date, including both affected 
and unaffected files.
3.	Test customer data must cover the following scenarios:
Criteria 1: Customer has a transaction associated in at least one source system 
- Bloomberg, Fidessa [*], Martini, or Murex - for the business date.
Criteria 2: Customer may be new and may not have any transaction associated 
for the business date.
2. Environment Preparation
1.	Compliance Technology Team to secure NON-PROD environments for:
o	Trade Surveillance
o	Siron AML/KYC
o	Intellimatch - requires a fresh engagement
2.	Compliance Technology Team to take database backups in the secured NON-PROD 
environment for:
o	Trade Surveillance
o	Siron AML/KYC
3.	Raise a request with the SQL DBA team to take PROD database backups for:
o	Trade Surveillance
o	Siron AML/KYC
4.	Raise a request with the SQL DBA team to restore the PROD databases into the 
secured NON-PROD test environment.
5.	Raise a data-borrowing request, obtain approvals, and obtain PROD data feeds for 
Business Dates T, T+1, T+2, …, T+N.
3. Testing Sequence - Business Date T
3.1 PROD Environment
1.	Wait for the PROD batch for Business Date T to complete.
2.	Collect and retain the PROD-generated input and output files for comparison.
3.2 TEST Environment
1.	Order the Trade Surveillance and Siron AML/KYC batches for Business Date T.
2.	Place the EDM-supplied files and the PROD-collected files for Business Date T into 
the \input\ folder.
3.	Start and run the Trade Surveillance batch.
4.	Collect the Trade Surveillance output files.
5.	Place the required output files into the Siron AML/KYC input folders.
6.	Run the Siron AML/KYC batch.
7.	Collect the Siron AML/KYC output files.
8.	Send the required files to Intellimatch for manual loading and report generation.
4. Testing Checks
4.1 Trade Surveillance Checks
REF
CHECK
A.1
Confirm that EDM files loaded successfully into the Staging Area via Control-M.
A.2
Compare EDM Staging tables in TEST against EDM Staging tables in PROD.
A.3
Compare EDM Central Model tables in TEST against EDM Central Model tables in 
PROD.
A.4
Compare Siron.Customer table in TEST against Siron.Customer table in PROD.
A.5
Compare SironHistory.Customer table in TEST against SironHistory.Customer table in PROD.
A.6
Compare the in_Customer file generated in TEST against the in_Customer file generated 
in PROD.
A.7
Compare all other in_* files generated in TEST against the corresponding in_* files 
generated in PROD.
A.8
Trace the data flow for changed records from EDM through to the generated output 
files.
4.2 Siron AML/KYC Checks
REF
CHECK
B.1
Compare GWG tables related to Customers in TEST against the equivalent GWG 
tables in PROD.
B.2
Business users to review the Siron GUI in TEST and compare the results against 
PROD.
4.3 Siron Reconciliation Checks
REF
CHECK
C.1
Compare reconciliation tables related to Customers in TEST against the equivalent 
reconciliation tables in PROD.
C.2
Compare the reconciliation outcomes between TEST and PROD.
4.4 Intellimatch Checks
REF
CHECK
D.1
Intellimatch team to manually run the batch in NON-PROD and share the generated 
reports.
D.2
Business users to review the reports and compare the results against PROD.
5. Repeat for Subsequent Business Dates
Repeat the full testing sequence and all checks in Sections 3 and 4 for each remaining 
business date: T+1, T+2, ... T+N.
6. Exit Criteria
Testing can be considered complete when:
*	All EDM files have loaded successfully through Control-M.
*	TEST and PROD comparisons show no unexplained differences.
*	Changed records can be traced end-to-end from EDM to the downstream output 
files.
*	Siron AML/KYC GUI results are reviewed and approved by the Business.
*	Siron reconciliation outcomes match expected PROD results and breaks are clear
*	Intellimatch reports are generated, reviewed, and approved by the Business.
*	All defects are logged, assessed, and either resolved or formally accepted.
7. Environment handover
Restore the database data with the backup taken before we started testing.
